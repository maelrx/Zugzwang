# Design research experiment

## Workflow

1. Formulate one primary causal question.
2. Identify prior work and the exact unresolved gap.
3. Decompose capability: perception, tracking, legal action, policy, value, search, explanation or system reliability.
4. Define conditions with `R/H/K`, observation profile and budget.
5. Build token/compute-matched controls and negative/placebo conditions.
6. Freeze sampling unit, pairing, randomization, exclusions and dataset hashes.
7. Define primary/secondary outcomes and evaluator provenance.
8. Simulate variance/power or state why pilot is exploratory.
9. Set hard budget, stopping and promotion rules before outcomes.
10. Write claim boundaries and preregistration amendment policy.

## Mandatory anti-confounds

- same model snapshot/date where comparison requires it;
- parsing error separate from illegal action and legal blunder;
- engine assistance separated from post-hoc evaluation;
- no transposition-near knowledge leakage;
- SAN/semantic legal list leakage documented;
- full-game promotion based on predeclared local criteria;
- paired openings/colors for match outcomes.
