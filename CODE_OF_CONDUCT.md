# Code of Conduct

## Standard

Contributors must communicate professionally, critique ideas and evidence rather than people, disclose conflicts of interest, and preserve research integrity. Harassment, discrimination, intimidation, doxxing, plagiarism, fabricated results and deliberate concealment of experimental conditions are unacceptable.

## Research-specific expectations

- credit datasets, papers and prior projects;
- disclose negative results and protocol deviations;
- do not weaponize private provider outputs or user data;
- distinguish fact, inference and opinion;
- resolve disputes through reproducible evidence where possible.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, reject or remove contributions and restrict participation. Serious incidents are escalated to the project owner. A more formal community code may replace this document when governance expands.
