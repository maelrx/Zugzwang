# Política de publicação científica

## Preconditions

- experiment card/preregistration frozen;
- bundle finalized and checksummed;
- artifact redistribution permitted;
- assistance and claims audits complete;
- primary outcomes analyzed before exploratory outcomes;
- exclusions, failures and missingness documented;
- uncertainty and paired sampling unit reported;
- exact source commit, schemas, providers and evaluators recorded.

## Claim ladder

| Term | Standard |
|---|---|
| Demonstrates | directly observed under published protocol |
| Supports | convergent evidence within stated regime |
| Suggests | plausible interpretation with alternatives open |
| Does not establish | explicit boundary against popular overclaim |

## Public artifacts

A public derivative bundle receives a new identity, transformation manifest and redaction report. It must not claim byte-equivalence to the private source bundle.

## Negative results

Null, negative and contradictory findings are publishable. Re-running only favorable seeds or conditions is prohibited unless reported as a separate exploratory analysis.
